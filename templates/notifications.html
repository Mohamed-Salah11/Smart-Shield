E-Mail

This is the FQDN or IP address of the SMTP E-Mail server to which notifications will be sent.

This is how many seconds it will wait for the SMTP server to connect. Default is 20s.

Sounds
When checked, emergency console messages, such as from a GUI login, will trigger a bell in connected consoles including serial terminals. On devices with a speaker, such messages can trigger an audible beep.
When this is checked, startup and shutdown sounds will no longer play through the built-in PC speaker. Has no effect on devices without a speaker.
